About

Careful hands, evidence-based plans

I'm a dental surgeon in Lahore. I currently manage the dental department at Cutera Aesthetics on my own, from the first consultation and treatment plan to follow-up, records and recalls.

Most of my day-to-day work is restorative and aesthetic: composite restorations, root canal treatment under rubber dam, whitening and smile makeovers. My growing focus is orthodontics. I'm completing a PGD in Orthodontics and I'm certified in clear aligner therapy by Alignofix and ClearPath.

Research shapes how I practise. I read the evidence before I plan, and I explain each option clearly so patients understand their treatment. Outside the clinic I've spent years organising screening camps, which taught me to lead teams and handle high patient volumes calmly.

Looking for: a clinical role in a practice where I can keep growing in aesthetic dentistry and orthodontics, including clear aligners.
